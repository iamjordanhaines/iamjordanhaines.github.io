# Jordan AI Uploader

Jordan AI Uploader is a small personal tool used only by Jordan Haines (YouTube: Jordan Haines and VIDEO GAME NERD).

It uses the official YouTube Data API to:

- upload videos to his own YouTube channels
- set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times on his own videos
- post and read comments on his own videos
- read basic statistics for his own channels

It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.
