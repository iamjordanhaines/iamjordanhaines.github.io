# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Jordan AI Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by Jordan Haines for his own YouTube channels only.

## What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these YouTube permissions:

- **youtube.upload**: to upload videos to the owner's own channels.
- **youtube.force-ssl**: to manage the owner's own videos and comments (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, publish times, comments) and read channel statistics.

## How the data is used and stored

- Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
- The sign-in key (refresh token) is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never uploaded to any server run by the app.
- The app has no database, no analytics and no advertising.
- No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.
- The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the key from the owner's computer also ends access.

## YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
